01Ownership

Your data. Your hardware. Full stop.

Every modern field-service platform wants your job history, client list and pricing living in their cloud, behind their subscription, on their terms. White Van Ops takes the opposite position: the entire system — application, web server and PostgreSQL database — installs on a computer you own.

  • 01
    The database ships in the installerIndustrial-grade PostgreSQL is bundled and configured automatically on first launch. No IT project, no separate download, no DBA.
  • 02
    No cloud dependencyInternet down? Vendor sunsets a product? Price-hike email? None of it can touch a system running in your own office.
  • 03
    Leave anytime, take everythingYour data sits in a standard PostgreSQL database with CSV exports built in. No export fees, no ransom, no proprietary lock-in.
  • 04
    Private by architectureEvery install gets its own session-signing secret and database credentials, generated on your machine. Field access runs through your own office connection — no middlemen, no subscriptions.
