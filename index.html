Sec. 07 Straight answers

The questions people actually ask

Buying software you host yourself raises fair questions. Here are the honest answers, including the ones that aren't flattering.

What happens if my office PC dies?

The app runs an automatic full backup every night to a mirror directory you choose — an external drive, a NAS, or a mapped network folder. You reinstall on a new PC and restore that backup. Keep the mirror on a different physical device than the app, and set it up on day one.

What if you go out of business?

Nothing stops working. The software is already installed on your machine and does not phone home to run — the licence is checked once at activation, offline afterwards. Your data sits in a standard PostgreSQL 17 database that any database professional can read, and every screen has a CSV export. There is no server of ours to switch off.

Is there a Mac or Linux version?

No. Windows only, today. The installer is built and tested for Windows 10 and 11. Your technicians' phones can be iPhone or Android — the field module runs in the phone's browser and installs to the home screen, so the office PC is the only Windows requirement.

Plus mentions a "secure tunnel" — isn't that the cloud you said you avoid?

Fair challenge, so here is the exact truth. Base uses no outside service at all: technicians' phones reach the office PC over your own WiFi and nothing leaves the building. Plus adds an encrypted tunnel so crews can work from anywhere. That tunnel carries traffic through a third party in encrypted form; it is a pipe, not a database. Your jobs, clients and pricing are still stored only on your PC. If you want zero outside involvement, choose Base.

Do my technicians need a signal to work?

No. The field module stores jobs on the phone and queues time entries and status changes while offline. When the phone is back in range — on Base, that means back on the office WiFi — the queue uploads by itself. Nobody has to remember to press sync.

Do I need an IT person to install it?

No, for the base install: it is one .exe, and the database installs and configures itself. Two things do need a few minutes of network work, and we document both — reserving a fixed address for the office PC on your router, and opening one Windows Firewall port so the phones can reach it. If you would rather not touch either, the $500 White Glove Installation covers it remotely while you watch.

What do I actually pay, and when?

Once. $1,000 for Base or $1,750 for Plus, and the licence is yours for life — there is no subscription and nothing renews. Base includes updates for the first year; the software keeps running whether or not you take updates after that. Installation and priority support are optional one-time add-ons, never required.

How many users and vehicles can I add?

As many as you want. There is no per-seat and no per-vehicle charge on either plan, so hiring a technician or buying a van never changes your bill. The practical ceiling is a single office location; multiple offices need a conversation.

Can I get my data out later?

Yes, and at no cost. CSV export is built into the product for invoices, time and records, and the underlying PostgreSQL database can be dumped whole with standard tools. There is no export fee and no proprietary file format.
